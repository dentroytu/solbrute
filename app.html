The gate is shut

Your wallet is your account here. Connect it and sign once to open the gate — no email, no password.

You need a Solana wallet

A wallet is a free browser extension that holds your account. SolBrute uses it instead of an email and password: nothing to remember, and no funds ever leave it.

Install Phantom

Install it, create your wallet and come back to this page. You don't need to put any money in.

What happens when you connect

  1. You pick a wallet and approve the connection. We read your public address, nothing else.
  2. You sign a short message to prove the address is yours. Signing is free and moves no funds.
  3. Your brutes load. They stay tied to that address.
