Features

What makes SolBrute different

🔗

Verifiable combat

Every fight is decided by a seed the server draws and publishes. Same seed, same fight: anyone can replay it and check.

🏛️

Player-driven economy

Arena rewards feed a real economy between the players themselves.

📈

Persistent progression

Your brute and its fight history are saved for good: nothing is lost between sessions.

🤝

Real PvP

You fight brutes created by other players, not just bots.

Roadmap
